# TEL Collection · Google audit (6 Oct 2026)

What Google can see of TEL, what it sends, and what Squires Ink has that TEL does not. Sources: Shopify (sessions by referrer since 1 Sep, product and page data, live theme head), Supermetrics (Google Business Profile for Squires Ink, last 30 days; connected-source inventory), live web searches run 6 Oct. The live site is blocked from this sandbox, so on-page checks come from the theme and the Shopify records, not a crawl.

## The one-line verdict

Google drives Squires because Squires has been introduced to Google: a Business Profile, reviews, directories, a studio site. TEL has none of that. It has one well-titled product page and a brand name Google already returns first. Everything else is missing, and all of it is free.

## What Google sees on the site

**Done well**

- A brand search returns telcollection.com.au first.
- The product page carries the right words where Google reads them: handle `tattoo-aftercare-kit`, SEO title "Tattoo Aftercare Kit | The Ritual Duo — TEL Collection", a meta description that says what it is. Keep the handle. It is the door.
- Product structured data via Shopify, Organization and WebSite schema on the home page, breadcrumbs, canonical tags, a hard-coded home meta description, a share image fallback, six product images with descriptive alt text, and empty collections kept out of the index. This is a tidy head.
- Judge.me premium can add review stars to the search result once rich snippets are switched on.

## The voice problem, and the rule that solves it

You do not want TEL to read as an aftercare brand. Google only sends people who type "tattoo aftercare". Both are true, and they do not conflict.

The rule: **the visible page speaks house, the invisible layer speaks category.** Title tags, meta descriptions, alt text, structured data, the Business Profile category and the Shopping feed carry the words Google needs. The hero, the headings and the copy on the page carry the words the house wants. A visitor from Google lands on "Earned. Not given." A visitor's search result read "Tattoo aftercare kit". Both are right. The product page already works this way. Extend it to every page.

## What this is worth

Search is the only channel that has converted above 5% for TEL. Squires' profile alone generates 2,230 views and 121 site visits a month for a studio. A TEL profile, a Shopping listing and one link from Squires could reasonably bring 150 to 300 search sessions a month within a quarter. At the 9% search conversion seen so far that is 10 to 25 online orders a month, more than online does in total today, from traffic that costs nothing. Treat that as an estimate, not a forecast. Search Console will tell us the real number.

## The plan, in order

**This week, about two hours, all free**

1. **Search Console.** Verify telcollection.com.au, submit the sitemap at /sitemap.xml. Everything after this is measurable.
2. **Google Business Profile for TEL Collection.** Category "Cosmetics store" or "Skin care products vendor". Two safe ways to list it beside Squires at the same address: as a separate business with its own name, phone line (info@ and a distinct number) and website, or as an online retailer with the address hidden and a service area of Australia. The second is cleaner and avoids a duplicate-address review. Add the Ritual Duo as a product, the six photos, the hours, and the link to the site.
3. **Google Shopping free listings.** Install Shopify's Google & YouTube channel, connect a new Merchant Center, approve the product. The Ritual Duo appears in the Shopping tab with price and, once Judge.me's feed is connected, stars.
4. **Judge.me rich snippets on.** Settings, then Rich Snippets. Stars under the product result.
5. **Search titles and descriptions on all seven pages.** Draft below.
6. **Three links from Squires.** Profile, site, Instagram. One sentence: "The aftercare we hand you at the counter."

**This month**

7. **FAQ schema** on the product FAQ and the FAQs page.
8. **The journal.** Four pieces to start, already half-written in the product copy: "Heavy work needs its own protocol", "Why two formulas", "Every ingredient, explained", "The first 48 hours". Search titles, house voice, one photograph each.
9. **Alt text** on the home page images in Shopify Files (hero scene, founder, the chair). Product images are done.
10. **Google reviews.** Every counter buyer who leaves a Judge.me review gets one line asking for the same on Google, once the profile exists.

**Hold**

- Google Ads for TEL. Same rule as Meta: no prospecting spend. The only exception worth watching is a competitor bidding on "TEL Collection", and there is no sign of that.
