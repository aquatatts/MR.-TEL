Why two

Two stages. Two formulas.

Fresh work needs sealing. Settling work needs conditioning. The balm is too heavy for the second stage; the cream too light for the first.

Step one · Days 1–14

Restore Balm

Richer · Sealing · Protective

A concentrated balm that lays down a protective layer and holds moisture in while the work closes over. Thin layer, clean dry skin, morning and night.

Step two · The months after

Recovery Cream

Calm · Condition · Refresh

A lighter daily cream that sinks in fast to hydrate and soothe while the work settles. The stage most people skip. Don't.

Heavy, detailed work uses both, layered. Fifteen years in the chair says one product can't do it. That is why they're sealed together.

What changed from v4

Nine sections, all purpose-built.

v4 was Prestige's stock sections dressed in your colours. v5 replaces them with sections written for this one product, so nothing on the page is a compromise with a theme built for clothing catalogues.

  • One metal, everywhere fixedv4 ran rose copper #DCA47B while the jars and box are gold foil. v5 restores gold #C9A24B across theme settings, layout, buttons, icons and every section. The site now matches the object in the customer's hand.
  • The hero is a scene, not a packshot newFull-bleed, 600px on mobile, an 18-second push-in, and a gradient veil that carries the type. The headline sits low-left where the thumb is, not floating in the middle.
  • A spec strip under the fold newOne mono line between hairlines: 60 ml × 2 · Sealed as a set · First run of five hundred. The facts land before any argument does.
  • The one light moment newThe product plate is the only pale surface on the page. On a black site that single frame does more work than any button.
  • A price ledger, not a price tag newAUD 59.95 set in Cormorant against a hairline rule, with free shipping beside it. Priced like a considered object, not a discount.
  • Two steps become a protocol newEach formula gets a labelled stage — Days 1–14, then the months after. It answers "when", which is the question people actually have.
  • Proof you can swipe newFive batch results as hairline-gold icons, scrolling sideways on the phone, five across on desktop. Claims with documents behind them.
  • The run as a numeral live data500 set at 140px in gold, then a sets-remaining meter wired to real Shopify inventory. It counts down on its own. Honest scarcity, no countdown-timer theatre.
  • Product page de-cluttered fixedVariant picker and quantity selector removed on a single-variant product. Eight accordions cut to six. Gold add-to-cart. The proof strip repeats below the gallery.
  • Every section is a setting editableHeadings, eyebrows, body copy, images, button labels, proof rows, run size — all editable in the theme editor without touching code.
