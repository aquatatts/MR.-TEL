3 · Website (v7.1, live)

Home page order: hero → founder → chair → aim → product → spec (off) → steps (off) → proof (off) → reviews → chapter → Chapter Two newsletter.

What works

  • The tone holds across hero, founder, chair and chapter. Nobody mistakes this for a generic aftercare store.
  • Announcement bar rotates three real messages. Judge.me, Klaviyo onsite and Clarity are all live.
  • The product page is complete: price, review badge, credential box, assurance, facts, seven accordions, steps, proof, reviews, capture, chair, FAQ.

What is costing orders

  1. Product is the fifth band. On a phone the Ritual Duo appears about four screens down. Move it to position two, or give the hero a direct "Shop the Duo" button to the product page.
  2. Spec, steps and proof are disabled. Those three bands answer "what is in it, how do I use it, why believe you". Proof scored as the strongest conversion element in the v6.1 audit. Turning them off made the page prettier and less persuasive.
  3. No sticky buy control on mobile. A slim bar (Ritual Duo · $59.95 · Shop) fixes this for free.
  4. Chapter Two newsletter competes with Chapter One. The page ends asking for a fragrance waitlist, not a purchase. Premature at 410 sets on hand.
  5. Stockists accordion with nothing behind it. See section 7.

No stockist or wholesale push. Aftercare is the door; the brand is online. TEL is a lifestyle house, and Chapter Two is a wearable fine fragrance, not a scent for a skincare product. The counter stays as the origin story and the proof engine. The list is the business.

The $14 figure is right. Packaging is more than half of it: formulas and jars are A$6.65, the presentation is A$5.50. The 2,000-set break only takes landed cost to about A$13.00, so it is not worth the cash tied up.

Cash to place the 1,000 run is about A$14,200, of which A$3,300 is the deposit. Revenue at full sell-through is A$54,500 ex GST. Gross margin about 75%.

Timing

410 sets at about 11 a week runs out around June 2027. Chinese New Year is 6 Feb 2027 and the factory has not committed to closure dates. To land before CNY the deposit needs to be paid by early December with samples already approved. The alternative is a March order landing by sea in May 2027. Decision date you set yourself: Fri 16 Oct.

Open risk: the reformulation

The 1 Oct email asks for a lighter, fragrance-free Restore Balm and a Recovery Cream without vanilla, colourant, PEG emulsifiers or methylparaben. Right direction for a brand that publishes its lists, but run two becomes a different product from the one with twelve five-star reviews. It needs client-tested samples, a composition sheet per product, new INCI on the jar, fresh COAs, and the preservative-efficacy and stability tests the factory does not hold (commission locally, ~A$1,500 to 3,000 a product, 8 to 12 weeks). Started now they finish mid-December, the same deadline as the pre-CNY deposit.
