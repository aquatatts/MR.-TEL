## 3. Findings

### 🔴 F-7 — The profile has one category, and it is the wrong noun

Category is **`Tattoo artist`**, with **no additional categories**.

Add up every term using *shop* or *shops* phrasing — `tattoo shops near me` (457), `tattoo shop`
(190), `tattoo shops gold coast` (127), `tattoo shops surfers paradise` (112),
`tattoo shop surfers paradise` (67), `surfers paradise tattoo shops` (56), `tattoo shop near me`
(49), `tattoo shop gold coast` (40), `walk in tattoo shops near me` (33),
`gold coast tattoo shops` (21):

> **1,152 of ~2,470 measurable impressions — roughly 47% — search for a *shop*, not an *artist*.**

Primary category is among the heaviest local ranking factors there is, and this one is fighting
its own demand. Add `Tattoo shop` (and `Tattoo parlour` if the listing offers it) as additional
categories. This is the single highest-leverage change available.

### 🔴 F-8 — Piercing demand is real and the profile does not claim it

`piercing` (20), `piercing gold coast` (16), plus sub-threshold `piercing surfers paradise`,
`piercing near me`, `piercing shops near me`, `piercing studio`, `body piercing gold coast`,
`piercing shop near meriton suites`, `open tattoos and piercing gold coast`.

Two possibilities, and they need opposite responses:

- **If the studio pierces** — add `Body piercing shop` as a category and a piercing service entry.
  Free demand currently going to competitors.
- **If it does not** — these impressions are diluting relevance and wasting the profile's
  authority on queries it can never satisfy. Worth noting rather than chasing.

Answer this one before acting on it.

### 🟠 F-9 — Bookings and messaging are switched off

**0 bookings and 0 messages in 90 days**, against 7,219 views and 163 phone calls.

For a studio that takes appointments and is open until 23:30 Friday and Saturday, both are pure
conversion left on the table — messaging in particular, because it catches the enquiry that
arrives at 10pm when nobody will pick up the phone. Enable messaging, and add a booking link.

### 🟠 F-10 — Website URL on the profile is HTTP

The profile points at **`http://www.squiresink.com/`**, not `https://`. Every one of those 529
website visits takes an extra redirect hop. Update it to the canonical HTTPS URL.

### 🟠 F-11 — Walk-ins are a measurable demand cluster with no home

`walk in tattoo surfers paradise` (48), `walk in tattoo shops near me` (33),
`tattoo gold coast walk in` (22) — **103 measurable impressions**, plus sub-threshold
`gold coast walk in tattoo` and `surfers paradise flash tattoo`.

The profile description mentions walk-ins and the reviews are full of them ("fitting a group of
four in on the day", "walk-ins get the same care as bookings"). There is almost certainly no
dedicated walk-ins page to rank for it. Given Meta already runs a Walk-Ins/Flash lane, this is a
paid priority with no organic counterpart.

### 🟠 F-12 — Fine line is a distinct cluster being served generically

`fine line tattoo gold coast` (38) measurable, plus sub-threshold `fine line tattoo`,
`fineline tattoo`, `fine line tattoo surfers paradise`, `fine line tattoo artist near me`,
`fine line & bespoke tattoo studio`. Reviews name Asia's fine line work specifically.

Deserves its own service page and a GBP service entry. Same pattern applies to rework/cover-ups,
which reviews describe repeatedly and which no keyword currently reaches.

### 🟡 F-13 — Review replies ran two months behind until 8 September

Response rate is excellent at 98%, but the timestamps show the reviews from 27 June through
4 September were nearly all answered in **one bulk sitting on 8 September**. Replies since then
have landed within 1–2 days.

So the gap was never willingness — it was latency, and it has already been fixed. Worth naming
only so it does not slip back: fresh replies carry more weight with both Google and the person
reading them than a backlog cleared at once.

### 🟡 F-14 — Maps is a fifth of views on a "near me"-dominated term set

Maps accounts for 1,440 of 7,219 views (20%), while the largest single cluster is *near me*
phrasing — `tattoo shops near me` (457), `tattoo near me` (99), `tattoo shop near me` (49),
`tattoos near me` (21), `walk in tattoo shops near me` (33) = **659 impressions**.

Those queries resolve in the map pack. A 20% Maps share against that much proximity intent is
worth watching once Search Console and a crawl are available to corroborate it.

### 🟢 Working well — protect these

- **354 reviews at 4.9, ~17 new per month.** That is the moat.
- **The replies are doing real SEO work.** They name artists (Augusto/NOLFIN, Valerio, Asia,
  Santiago), name the street, and repeat "tattoo studio in Surfers Paradise" — all indexable
  entity and geo signal, written naturally rather than stuffed. Keep this exactly as it is.
- **The profile description is genuinely good** — categories of work, five artists, seven days,
  late weekends, licensed, single-use needles, vegan inks, and a cross-sell to TEL aftercare.
- **~97% non-branded discovery** means the listing is earning its traffic on merit.

## 4. One thing the data surfaced that nobody asked about

Several reviews are explicitly holiday traffic: *"great tattoos on holidays"*, *"see you next
holiday session"*, *"during the busy season"*, *"come back when you're next in Surfers"*.

Surfers Paradise is a tourist strip and the review corpus says a real share of the chair is
visitors, not locals. None of the current keyword coverage speaks to it — nothing about holiday
tattoos, tourist walk-ins, or "getting tattooed on the Gold Coast while you're here". That is an
untouched content angle with demonstrated demand, and it pairs naturally with the walk-in page
in F-11.

## 5. Suggested order

1. **F-7** — add `Tattoo shop` as a category. Cheapest change, largest expected effect.
2. **F-8** — settle the piercing question, then act on it in whichever direction is true.
3. **F-9** — enable messaging; add a booking link.
4. **F-10** — switch the profile URL to HTTPS.
5. **F-11 / F-12** — walk-ins and fine line service pages, once the site is reachable.
6. **F-13** — keep replies inside 48 hours.

Items 1–4 are all Business Profile changes and need no developer, no crawl access, and no
Search Console.
